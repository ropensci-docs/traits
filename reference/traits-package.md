# traits - Species trait data from around the web

Currently included in `traits` with the associated function name or
function prefix:

## Details

- BETYdb http://www.betydb.org - `betydb_`

- National Center for Biotechnology Information - NCBI
  http://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/ - `ncbi_`

- Encyclopedia of Life Traitbank - `traitbank_`

- Birdlife International https://www.birdlife.org/ - `birdlife_`

- LEDA Traitbase http://www.leda-traitbase.org/LEDAportal/index.jsp -
  `leda_`

- Zanne et al. plant dataset -
  [`tr_zanne()`](https://docs.ropensci.org/traits/reference/tr_zanne.md)

- Amniote life history dataset -
  [`tr_ernest()`](https://docs.ropensci.org/traits/reference/tr_ernest.md)

See also
[traits-defunct](https://docs.ropensci.org/traits/reference/traits-defunct.md)
